Unblockable cookies

The Unblockable frontend does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies.

Browser storage

Locally hosted games may use localStorage or similar browser storage for game state and high scores. Browser storage is not the same thing as a traditional tracking cookie, but it persists on the device until cleared.

External games

Official third-party games may set their own cookies or use other storage after you choose to load or open them. Those technologies are controlled by the publisher, not Unblockable.

Your controls

You can clear site data and cookies from your browser settings. Doing so may erase local game progress.
